# Settings

## What is this?

The Settings page lets you manage your account, profile, security, billing, business details, and app preferences.

## Why it is useful

Settings give you control over how Athli works for you. From updating your profile to managing your subscription, everything is accessible from one place.

## Settings Sections

### Account > Profile
Update your display name, profile picture, and timezone.

1. Go to **Settings > Account > Profile**
2. Edit your name, upload a new profile picture, or change your timezone
3. Click **Save**

> [Screenshot: Profile settings page with name and timezone fields]

### Account > Security
Manage your email address, password, and security settings.

1. Go to **Settings > Account > Security**
2. Change your email (requires verification)
3. Update your password

> [Screenshot: Security settings page]

### Account > Information
View your account details and membership information.

### Billing
Manage your subscription, view invoices, and update payment methods. See the [Plans and Billing](../getting-started/04-plans-and-billing.md) guide for details.

### Business > Company Information
Set up your coaching business details including business name, description, and branding.

1. Go to **Settings > Business > Company Information**
2. Enter your business name and description
3. Upload your logo and set brand colors

> [Screenshot: Company information settings page]

### Business > Branding
Customize the visual appearance of your client-facing materials.

### Business > Team
Manage team members if you operate a coaching business with multiple staff.

### App > Customisations
Configure how the app behaves for your coaching style.

1. Go to **Settings > App > Customisations**
2. Choose which exercise columns to show in workouts (sets, reps, weight, RPE, tempo, etc.)
3. Set your preferred defaults

> [Screenshot: App customisations page with column toggles]

### Notifications
Control what notifications you receive and how you receive them.

1. Go to **Settings > Notifications**
2. Toggle notifications on or off for different event types
3. Choose between push, email, or both

> [Screenshot: Notification preferences page]

### Danger Zone
Account deletion and irreversible actions.

1. Go to **Settings > Account > Danger**
2. This section contains destructive actions like account deletion
3. Proceed with extreme caution

> [Screenshot: Danger zone settings with delete account option]

## Things to Note

- Changing your email requires verification of the new address
- Business branding settings affect what your clients see
- Timezone settings affect workout scheduling and check-in due dates
- Notification settings apply across web and mobile

---

## FAQs

### Can I change my email address?

Yes. Go to **Settings > Account > Security** and update your email. You will need to verify the new address.

### How do I cancel my account?

Go to **Settings > Account > Danger** and follow the cancellation process. Your data is preserved in case you want to return.

### Can I use a custom domain?

Custom domains are not currently supported.

### How do I export my data?

Contact support for data export requests.
