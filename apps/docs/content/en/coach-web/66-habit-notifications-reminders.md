# Habit Notifications and Reminders

## What is this?

When creating or editing a habit, you can configure the duration and notification settings. These control how long the habit runs and whether clients receive push notification reminders to log their habits.

## Why is it useful?

Timely reminders improve habit adherence. Without notifications, clients may forget to log their habits. Setting a duration ensures habits do not run indefinitely unless you want them to.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Configuring Duration and Notifications

1. Go to **Library > Habits**
2. Click **Create Habit** or edit an existing habit
3. Set the **duration** (Days, Months, or Years) to control how long the habit stays active
4. Toggle **notifications** on or off to control whether clients receive push reminders
5. Click **Save**

## Things to Note

- Push notifications require the client to have the Athli app installed and notifications enabled on their device
- Clients can manage their notification preferences in the mobile app settings
- Notifications are sent based on the tracking period (daily or weekly)

## FAQs

### What if a client turns off notifications?

They will not receive push reminders but can still log habits manually by opening the app.

### Do notifications work on both iOS and Android?

Yes. Push notifications are supported on both platforms as long as they are enabled in the device settings.
