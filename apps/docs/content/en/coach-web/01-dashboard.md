# Home Dashboard

## What is this?

The Home Dashboard is the first thing you see when you log in. It gives you a snapshot of what is happening across your coaching business today.

## Why it is useful

Instead of checking each client individually, the dashboard surfaces the most important information so you can quickly see who needs attention, what workouts are scheduled, and what tasks are pending.

## Use Cases

- Starting your day by reviewing which clients have workouts scheduled
- Spotting at-risk clients who have missed workouts or check-ins
- Checking completed workout summaries and client feedback
- Managing your daily to-do list

## Dashboard Cards

### Today's Workouts
Shows all client workouts scheduled for today. Each card displays the client name, workout name, completion status, and pre-workout metrics (sleep, mood, energy, stress, soreness) if the client has logged them.

> [Screenshot 1: Today's workouts card showing client workout cards with status indicators]

### At-Risk Clients
Highlights clients who may need your attention based on missed workouts, overdue check-ins, or declining engagement.

> [Screenshot 2: At-risk clients card with warning indicators]

### Completed Workouts
Shows recently completed workouts with client feedback and post-workout ratings. You can filter by status (completed, in progress, missed).

> [Screenshot 3: Completed workouts card with filter dropdown]

### To-Do List
Your personal task list. Shows both automated tasks from Athli (like check-in reviews) and custom tasks you have added.

> [Screenshot 4: To-do card with task items and checkboxes]

### Summary Cards
Quick stats at the top showing key numbers like active clients, workouts completed today, and pending check-ins.

> [Screenshot 5: Summary cards row at top of dashboard]

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing a Client's Workout Details

1. Find the workout card on the dashboard
2. Click on it to open the workout preview
3. You can see all exercises, sets, reps, and any notes the client added
4. Click **View Profile** to go to the client's full profile

> [Screenshot 6: Workout preview dialog with exercise details]

### Messaging a Client from the Dashboard

1. Find the client's workout card
2. Click the **Message** button
3. A quick message input appears
4. Type your message and send

> [Screenshot 7: Quick message input on workout card]

## Things to Note

- The dashboard updates in real time as clients complete workouts
- Pre-workout metrics (sleep, mood, etc.) are submitted by the client before starting their workout
- The to-do list syncs between web and mobile

## FAQs

### Can I customize what appears on the dashboard?

The dashboard shows a fixed set of cards that are designed to surface the most important daily information. Custom dashboards are not available yet.

### Why are some clients not showing on the dashboard?

Only clients with workouts scheduled for today appear in the workout section. At-risk clients appear based on engagement patterns.

### Can I see previous days?

The dashboard focuses on today. For historical data, go to the individual client's profile.
