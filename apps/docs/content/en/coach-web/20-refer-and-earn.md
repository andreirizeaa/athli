# Refer and Earn

## What is this?

The Refer and Earn program lets you earn rewards by inviting other coaches to join Athli. When someone signs up using your referral link or code, you both benefit.

## Why it is useful

If you know other coaches who would benefit from Athli, referring them earns you credits or rewards. It is a simple way to give back to the coaching community while getting something in return.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Getting Your Referral Link

1. Click **Refer and Earn** in the sidebar
2. Your unique referral link is displayed
3. Click **Copy Link** to copy it to your clipboard
4. Share it with other coaches via email, social media, or messaging

> [Screenshot 1: Refer and earn page with referral link and copy button]

### Getting Your Referral Code

1. On the same page, you can also find your referral code
2. Share this code for coaches who prefer entering a code during signup

> [Screenshot 2: Referral code section]

### Tracking Your Referrals

1. The Refer and Earn page shows your referral statistics
2. See how many people signed up using your link
3. Track your earned rewards

> [Screenshot 3: Referral stats showing signups and rewards]

## Things to Note

- Referral links are unique to your account
- Both you and the referred coach receive benefits
- Check the page for current reward details as they may change over time

---

## FAQs

### Who can I refer?

Any coach who does not already have an Athli account.

### When do I receive my reward?

Rewards are credited when the referred coach creates their account and meets the qualifying criteria.

### Is there a limit on how many people I can refer?

No. Refer as many coaches as you want.
