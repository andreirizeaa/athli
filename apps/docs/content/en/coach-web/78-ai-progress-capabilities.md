# AI for Progress and Analytics

## What is this?

The AI Assistant can pull up client progress data, analyze trends, and generate visual charts. You can ask about metrics, check-in history, exercise performance, and habit adherence without navigating to each client's profile individually.

## Why is it useful?

Tracking client progress across multiple data points is time-consuming when done manually. The AI gives you instant access to progress summaries, trend analysis, and visual charts by simply asking. It is especially valuable for quick client check-ups before sessions and end-of-week reviews across your roster.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Checking Client Metrics

Ask the AI to show recent metric values and trends:

- **"What are Sarah's latest metrics?"** - Shows recent values and directional trends
- **"Show me Jake's body weight over the last 3 months"** - Displays metric history
- **"What is Sarah's current body fat percentage?"** - Pulls a specific metric

> [Screenshot 1: AI showing client metrics with recent values and trend indicators]

### Reviewing Check-In History

Ask the AI to pull up check-in submissions:

- **"Show me Jake's check-in history"** - Displays recent check-in responses
- **"What did Sarah report in her last check-in?"** - Shows the most recent submission
- **"How has Jake's energy level been this month?"** - Tracks a specific check-in field over time

> [Screenshot 2: AI displaying check-in history with response summaries]

### Analyzing Exercise Performance

Ask the AI about workout and exercise progress:

- **"How is Sarah's squat progressing?"** - Shows exercise history with weight and rep trends
- **"What is Jake's bench press one-rep max trend?"** - Tracks strength progression
- **"Show me Sarah's deadlift history"** - Lists exercise performance over time

### Checking Habit Adherence

Ask the AI to report on habit completion:

- **"What are Jake's habit completion rates?"** - Shows adherence data across all habits
- **"How consistent has Sarah been with her water intake habit?"** - Checks a specific habit
- **"Which clients have low habit completion this week?"** - Identifies clients who need attention

> [Screenshot 3: AI showing habit completion rates with percentage breakdowns]

### Running Comprehensive Analysis

Ask the AI for a full progress overview:

- **"Analyze Sarah's progress over the last month"** - Comprehensive analysis across workouts, metrics, and habits
- **"Give me a summary of Jake's progress this week"** - Quick weekly overview
- **"How is Sarah doing overall?"** - General progress check

### Generating Visual Charts

Ask the AI to create charts from metric data:

- **"Generate a chart of Jake's body weight"** - Creates a visual line chart of the metric over time
- **"Chart Sarah's squat progress"** - Visualizes exercise performance

> [Screenshot 4: AI-generated line chart showing body weight trend over time]

## Things to Note

- The AI reads from data already tracked in each client's profile
- Progress analysis is only as good as the data available; ensure clients are logging consistently
- Charts are generated from metric data and displayed directly in the chat
- The AI can compare trends across different time periods when asked
- Great for preparing for client meetings or end-of-week review sessions

## FAQs

### Can the AI compare two clients' progress?

You can ask about multiple clients in the same conversation, but the AI does not generate side-by-side comparison charts.

### How far back can the AI look at data?

The AI can access all historical data stored in the client's profile, going back to when tracking started.

### Can the AI predict future progress?

No. The AI reports on existing data and trends. It does not make forward projections.

### What if a client has no data yet?

The AI will let you know that no data is available for the requested metric or time period.
