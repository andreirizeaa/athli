# AI for Communication

## What is this?

The AI Assistant can draft messages to your clients based on context, progress data, and your instructions. Whether you need a check-in reminder, workout feedback, or motivational note, the AI writes it and presents it for your review before sending.

## Why is it useful?

Writing personalized messages to every client takes time, especially when you have a large roster. The AI drafts contextual messages quickly, saving you time while keeping communication personal. Since you review every message before it is sent, you stay in full control of what your clients receive.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Drafting Messages

Ask the AI to write messages for specific clients:

- **"Draft a message to Sarah about her workout today"** - Prepares a message based on recent workout data
- **"Write a check-in reminder for Jake"** - Drafts a friendly reminder to complete a check-in
- **"Send an encouraging message to Sarah about hitting her PR"** - Creates a motivational message using performance context

> [Screenshot: AI draft message action card with message content and send button]

### Reviewing and Sending

1. The AI shows a **draft message** in an action card
2. Read the message content carefully
3. Click **Confirm** to send the message to the client
4. Click **Edit** to modify the message before sending
5. Click **Dismiss** to cancel without sending

All messages are drafts until you explicitly confirm. The AI never sends messages directly.

> [Screenshot: Action card with Confirm, Edit, and Dismiss options for a draft message]

### Types of Messages You Can Request

- **Workout feedback** - Comment on completed sessions
- **Check-in reminders** - Prompt clients to submit check-ins
- **Motivational messages** - Celebrate milestones and PRs
- **Progress updates** - Summarize recent achievements
- **General communication** - Any custom message you describe

### Using Context in Messages

The AI can incorporate client data into messages:

- Recent workout completions and performance
- Metric trends and progress
- Habit adherence data
- Upcoming scheduled workouts
- Client goals and milestones

Ask something like "Write a message to Jake congratulating him on improving his squat by 10kg this month" and the AI pulls the relevant data to craft a personalized note.

## Things to Note

- All messages are drafts that require your confirmation before sending
- The AI does not send messages to clients directly under any circumstance
- Messages are sent through the Athli messaging system
- The AI can also create check-in templates and assign metrics through action cards
- You can ask the AI to adjust the tone (more formal, more casual, shorter, longer)
- The AI uses available client data to add relevant context to messages

## FAQs

### Can the AI send messages without my approval?

No. Every message is presented as a draft in an action card. You must click Confirm to send it.

### Can I edit the message before sending?

Yes. Click **Edit** on the action card to modify the message text before confirming.

### Can the AI message multiple clients at once?

You can ask the AI to draft messages for multiple clients in the same conversation. Each message gets its own action card for individual review and confirmation.

### Does the AI read my past messages with clients?

No. The AI does not have access to your message history. It drafts new messages based on client data and your instructions.

### Can I ask the AI to write in a specific style?

Yes. Include style instructions in your request, such as "Write a casual and friendly message" or "Keep it short and professional."
