# Alternate Exercises

## What is this?

Alternate exercises are backup options you can assign to any exercise in a workout. If a client cannot perform the primary exercise due to equipment availability, injury, or preference, they can switch to the designated alternate directly in the mobile app. This gives clients flexibility without needing to message you for a substitution.

## Why is it useful?

Clients train in different environments. A client who usually trains at a fully equipped gym may occasionally work out at a hotel gym or at home. By providing alternate exercises, you ensure the workout intent is preserved regardless of circumstances. It also reduces back-and-forth communication about substitutions and empowers clients to stay on track independently.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Setting an Alternate Exercise

1. Open the Workout Builder and locate the exercise you want to add an alternate for.
2. Click on the exercise card to expand its options, or click the **three-dot menu** on the exercise.
3. Select **Set Alternate Exercise** from the menu.
4. The exercise picker opens. Search for or browse to the exercise you want as the alternate.
5. Select the alternate exercise. It is now linked to the primary exercise.
6. The alternate exercise appears below or beside the primary exercise with a visual indicator (e.g., "Alt" label).

## Things to Note

- Each primary exercise can have one alternate exercise assigned.
- Alternate exercises can be any exercise from your library, including custom exercises.
- Alternates do not affect the primary exercise's tracking or programming. They exist purely as a fallback option.
- Consider choosing alternates that target the same muscle groups and movement patterns as the primary exercise for best results.

## FAQs

**Can I set multiple alternates for one exercise?**
Currently, each exercise supports one alternate. If you need more options, consider adding a note to the exercise listing additional alternatives.

**Does the client have to use the alternate?**
No. The alternate is optional. Clients can always perform the primary exercise or choose the alternate at their discretion.

**Can I set the same alternate for multiple exercises?**
Yes. The same exercise can be used as an alternate for different primary exercises across the workout.
