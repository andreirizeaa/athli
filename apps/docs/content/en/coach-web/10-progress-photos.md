# Progress Photos

## What is this?

Progress photos let clients upload photos on a regular basis so you can visually track their physical transformation. You can compare photos from different dates side by side.

## Why it is useful

Numbers do not always tell the full story. A client's weight might stay the same while their body composition improves dramatically. Progress photos give you visual proof of change that you can share with your client for motivation.

## Use Cases

- Monthly physique progress tracking
- Before and after comparisons for program evaluations
- Visual documentation for client testimonials (with permission)
- Tracking posture improvements over time

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing Client Photos

1. Go to the client's profile
2. Click the **Photos** tab
3. You see a calendar or date-based list of uploaded photos
4. Click on a date to view the photos from that day

> [Screenshot: Photos tab with date-based photo grid]

### Adding Photos on Behalf of a Client

1. On the client's Photos tab, click **Add Photo**
2. Upload an image from your computer
3. Select the date
4. Click **Save**

> [Screenshot: Add photo dialog with upload area]

### Comparing Photos

1. On the Photos tab, click **Compare**
2. Select two dates to compare
3. Photos from each date appear side by side
4. Swipe or scroll between photos

> [Screenshot: Photo comparison view with two dates side by side]

## Things to Note

- Clients can upload photos directly from the mobile app
- Photos are stored securely and only visible to you and the client
- Image files must be under 5MB
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG

## Common Problems

**Photos are not uploading**
Check the file size (must be under 5MB) and format (JPG or PNG only).

**I want to delete a photo**
Open the photo and use the delete option. This cannot be undone.

## FAQs

### Can clients see their own progress photos?

Yes. Clients can view and upload photos from their mobile app.

### Are photos backed up?

Yes. Photos are stored securely in the cloud and accessible from any device.

### Can I download a client's photos?

Yes. Use the download option on any individual photo.

### Can I compare more than two dates?

The comparison tool currently supports two dates at a time.
