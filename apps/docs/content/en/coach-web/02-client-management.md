# Client Management

## What is this?

The Athletes page is where you manage all your coaching clients. You can view, search, filter, add, archive, and organize your entire client roster.

## Why it is useful

As your coaching business grows, you need a central place to manage all your clients. The Athletes page gives you a bird's eye view of your roster and quick access to each client's profile, training, and progress.

## Use Cases

- Viewing all your active clients at a glance
- Searching for a specific client by name
- Adding new clients individually or in bulk
- Archiving clients who are no longer active
- Restoring previously archived clients

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing Your Client List

1. Click **Athletes** in the sidebar
2. You will see a list of all your active clients
3. Each client card shows their name, profile picture, category, and status
4. Use the search bar at the top to find specific clients

> [Screenshot 1: Athletes page with client list and search bar]

### Adding a New Client

1. Click the **Add Client** button in the top right
2. Fill in the client's details (name, email, category)
3. Optionally add goals, notes, and other information
4. Click **Send Invite**
5. The client receives an email invitation to join

> [Screenshot 2: Add client side panel with form]

### Viewing a Client's Profile

1. Click on any client from the list
2. You land on their **Overview** page
3. From here, navigate to different sections using the tabs:
   - **Overview** - Bio, goals, injuries, recent workouts
   - **Training** - Weekly calendar with assigned workouts
   - **Check-ins** - Submitted check-in forms
   - **Habits** - Assigned habits and completion logs
   - **Metrics** - Custom tracked metrics with charts
   - **Photos** - Progress photos with comparison
   - **Exercise History** - Performance data per exercise
   - **Questionnaires** - Completed questionnaire submissions
   - **Files** - Shared documents and files
   - **Notes** - Private coaching notes
   - **Settings** - Client-specific settings

> [Screenshot 3: Client overview page with navigation tabs]

### Editing Client Details

1. Go to the client's profile
2. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon)
3. Update their name, email, profile picture, category, or timezone
4. Click **Save**

> [Screenshot 4: Edit client details side panel]

### Archiving a Client

1. Go to the client's profile
2. Click **Settings** in the navigation tabs
3. Click **Archive Client**
4. Confirm the action
5. The client will no longer appear in your active list and will lose app access

> [Screenshot 5: Client settings page with archive option]

### Restoring Archived Clients

1. On the Athletes page, click the **Restore** button
2. A panel shows all archived clients
3. Select the client(s) you want to restore
4. Click **Restore**
5. They regain access to the app immediately

> [Screenshot 6: Restore clients side panel]

### Uploading Clients via CSV

1. Click the **Upload** button on the Athletes page
2. Download the CSV template
3. Fill in your client data (name, email, category)
4. Upload the completed file
5. Review and confirm the import

> [Screenshot 7: CSV upload side panel with preview]

## Client Profile Sections

### Overview
The overview page shows a summary of the client including their bio, goals, injuries, and recent workout activity.

### Training
A weekly calendar view where you can assign workouts, apply programs, and see completion status for each day.

### Goals and Injuries
Track client goals (e.g., "Lose 5kg", "Run a marathon") and any injuries that affect their training.

## Things to Note

- Archived clients do not count toward your plan's active client limit
- Each client needs a unique email address
- Changing a client's email will require them to verify the new address
- You can resend invite emails from the client's settings page if they did not receive the original

## Common Problems

**A client is not appearing in my list**
Check if they are archived. Also make sure they accepted the invite and created their account.

**I accidentally archived a client**
You can restore them immediately from the Restore panel. Their data is preserved.

**The CSV upload is failing**
Make sure your file matches the template format exactly. Check for extra columns, special characters, or encoding issues.

## FAQs

### How many clients can I have?

This depends on your plan. Check **Settings > Billing** for your current limit.

### Can I organize clients into groups?

Client categories (Online, Hybrid, In Person) help you filter your roster. Custom groups are not available yet.

### Can I see when a client was last active?

The client list shows activity indicators. You can also check the At-Risk section on your dashboard.

### Can I transfer a client to another coach?

This is not currently supported. The client would need to be invited by the other coach separately.
