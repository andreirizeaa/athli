# MuscleWiki Integration

## What is this?

Built-in exercises in the Workout Builder include professional video demonstrations sourced from MuscleWiki, a widely used open exercise reference. These videos show proper form and technique for each movement. The integration works automatically with no setup required.

## Why is it useful?

- See proper form demonstrations for exercises while building workouts
- Reduces the need to film and upload your own videos for common movements
- Professional quality videos that are consistent and reliable
- Zero setup required: videos are attached to built-in exercises out of the box

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing Exercise Videos

1. Open the **Workout Builder** from any workout or programme.
2. In the left panel, click on any built-in exercise.
3. The exercise detail shows the MuscleWiki video demonstration.
4. Play the video to review the form and technique.

## Things to Note

- MuscleWiki videos are attached to built-in exercises only. Custom exercises require you to upload your own media.
- Built-in exercises and their videos are read-only. If you want a different video for a specific movement, duplicate the exercise and upload your own.
- Custom exercises do not have MuscleWiki videos. You can upload your own video or image when creating a custom exercise.

## FAQs

### Can I replace a MuscleWiki video with my own?

Not directly on a built-in exercise, since they are read-only. You can duplicate the exercise and upload your own video to the copy.

### What if a built-in exercise does not have a video?

The vast majority of built-in exercises include video demonstrations. If one does not, you can duplicate it and upload your own video.
