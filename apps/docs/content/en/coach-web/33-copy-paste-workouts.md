# Copying and Pasting Workouts

## What is this?

The Workout Builder allows you to duplicate existing workouts, save workouts to your library for reuse, and copy workouts between different clients. This means you can create a workout once and apply it to multiple clients or programmes without rebuilding it from scratch each time.

## Why is it useful?

Many coaches use similar workout templates across clients, adjusting only the weights or rep ranges. Being able to duplicate and reuse workouts saves significant time, especially when onboarding new clients or building periodised programmes. It also ensures consistency across your coaching practice.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Duplicating a Workout from the Training Grid

1. Navigate to **Library > Training > Workouts** to open the training grid.
2. Select one or more workouts using the checkboxes.
3. Click the **Duplicate** button that appears in the bulk actions bar.
4. A copy of each selected workout is created with all exercises, sets, reps, weights, and section configurations intact.
5. Open the duplicated workout and make any necessary adjustments (e.g., changing weights or exercise substitutions).

> [Screenshot 1: Training grid with workout checkboxes selected and the Duplicate bulk action button]

### Saving a Workout to Your Library from a Client's Calendar

1. Go to the client's **Training** tab and open their training calendar.
2. Click on the workout you want to save.
3. Click **Save to Library**.
4. The workout is now stored in your workout library, accessible when building programmes for any client.

> [Screenshot 2: Client training calendar with a workout open showing the Save to Library option]

## Things to Note

- Duplicated workouts are independent copies. Editing one does not affect the other.
- Library workouts serve as templates. Each time you add one to a client, it creates a new instance.
- When copying between clients, exercise configurations (sets, reps, weights) are preserved, but you should review and adjust weights for the individual client.
- Custom exercises used in the workout are also included in the copy, provided they exist in your exercise library.
- Workout notes and section configurations are preserved during duplication.

## FAQs

**Does duplicating a workout copy the section types too?**
Yes. All section types (AMRAP, EMOM, Circuit, etc.) and their configurations are preserved in the duplicate.

**Can I share a workout with another coach?**
Workout sharing between coach accounts is not currently supported. Each coach manages their own workout library independently.

**If I update a library workout, do existing copies update?**
No. Library workouts are templates. Once added to a client's programme, they are independent instances. Updates to the library version do not propagate to existing copies.

**Can I duplicate an entire programme, not just a single workout?**
Yes. Programme duplication is handled at the programme level, which copies all workouts within the programme. See the programme management articles for details.
