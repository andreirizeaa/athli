# Check-ins

## What is this?

Check-ins are recurring forms that you assign to clients to collect regular feedback. Clients fill them out on a schedule (daily, weekly, etc.) and you can review their responses, track trends, and compare submissions over time.

## Why it is useful

Regular check-ins keep you informed about how your clients are feeling, recovering, and progressing. Instead of relying on clients to message you updates, check-ins create a structured feedback loop that makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.

## Use Cases

- Weekly progress check-ins asking about training, nutrition, and recovery
- Daily wellness check-ins tracking mood, sleep, and energy
- Monthly body measurement submissions
- Post-program feedback surveys

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Creating a Check-in Template

1. Go to **Library > Forms > Check-ins**
2. Click **Create Check-in**
3. Give it a name and description
4. Add questions using different types:
   - **Text** for open-ended answers
   - **Number** for measurements
   - **Rating** for scale-based feedback (e.g., 1-5 stars)
   - **Yes/No** for simple binary questions
   - **Multiple Choice** for predefined options
   - **Scale** for slider-based input
5. Mark questions as required or optional
6. Click **Save**

> [Screenshot 2: Add question side panel showing all available question types]

### Assigning a Check-in to a Client

1. Go to the client's profile
2. Click the **Check-ins** tab
3. Click **Assign Check-in**
4. Select a check-in template from your library
5. Set the frequency (daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly)
6. Choose the start date
7. Click **Assign**

> [Screenshot 3: Assign check-in dialog with frequency options]

### Reviewing Submissions

When a client submits a check-in, it appears in the **Submissions** tab on the check-in detail page. This tab shows a list of all submissions on the left sidebar with their dates and review status.

1. Go to the client's **Check-ins** tab
2. Click on the assigned check-in to open its detail page
3. Select the **Submissions** tab
4. Click any submission in the left sidebar to view the full responses
5. Each submission shows the questions with the client's answers displayed in a card format

> [Screenshot 4: Check-in submissions tab with sidebar and response card]

### Reviewing a Check-in as a Coach

After viewing a client's check-in submission, you can leave a review with your feedback. This lets the client know you have seen their responses and provides a space for coaching notes.

1. Open a check-in submission from the **Submissions** tab
2. Scroll down below the response card to the review section
3. Write your feedback or coaching notes in the text area
4. Click **Submit Review**
5. The submission status updates to **Reviewed**

The client can see your review in their mobile app, keeping the feedback loop closed.

> [Screenshot 5: Coach review section below a check-in response]

### Comparing Check-ins Over Time

The **Compare** tab lets you view two submissions side by side to track changes over time. This is useful for spotting trends in a client's responses across different periods.

1. Open a check-in detail page
2. Select the **Compare** tab
3. Two cards appear side by side, each with a date selector
4. Choose different submission dates for the left and right cards
5. The responses for each date are displayed beneath the selector, making it easy to compare answers question by question

> [Screenshot 6: Check-in compare tab with two submissions side by side]

### Downloading Check-in Submissions as PDF

You can download any check-in submission as a PDF. The download button is available on all tabs when at least one completed submission exists.

1. Click the **Download** button in the top-right of the check-in detail page
2. Select **Latest** to download the most recent submission
3. Or select a specific submission date from the dropdown list
4. The PDF includes the form name, client details, completion date, all questions, and the client's answers

> [Screenshot 7: Download dropdown showing submission dates]

### Reviewing Check-ins from the Dashboard

1. Go to the **Check-ins** page from the sidebar
2. This shows all pending and recent check-in submissions across all clients
3. Click any submission to review it directly

> [Screenshot 8: Check-ins review page with submissions from multiple clients]

## Things to Note

- Clients receive a notification when a check-in is due
- You can edit check-in templates at any time, but changes do not affect already-submitted responses
- Check-in data is visible to both coach and client
- Automation flows can trigger actions based on missed check-ins
- The download button is visible on all tabs (Builder, Submissions, Compare) as long as there is at least one completed submission

## Common Problems

**The client says they did not receive a check-in notification**
Check that push notifications are enabled on their device. Also verify the check-in frequency and start date.

**I want to change the questions on a check-in that is already assigned**
Edit the template in the Library. New submissions will use the updated questions. Past submissions are not affected.

## FAQs

### Can I assign multiple check-ins to the same client?

Yes. You can assign different check-in templates with different frequencies.

### Can clients submit check-ins late?

Yes. Clients can fill in a check-in after the due date.

### Can I see trends across multiple check-ins?

Use the Compare tab to view two submissions side by side and track changes question by question.

### Can I export check-in data?

Yes. Use the Download button on any tab to export a submission as a PDF. You can choose the latest or any specific submission date.
