# Browsing Exercises

## What is this?

There are two places to browse exercises in Athli: the full built-in library inside the Workout Builder, and your custom exercises list under Library > Training > Exercises.

## Why is it useful?

- Browse thousands of built-in exercises with video demonstrations when building workouts
- Manage your custom exercises in a dedicated list

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Full Exercise List in the Workout Builder

The complete exercise library (including all built-in exercises) is available in the left panel of the **Workout Builder**. Open any workout or programme and enter the Workout Builder to access it. You can search by name or filter by muscle group and equipment.

> [Screenshot 1: Workout Builder left panel showing the full exercise library with search and filters]

### Custom Exercises in Library > Training > Exercises

To view and manage your custom exercises, go to **Library > Training > Exercises**. This page lists only exercises you have created. From here you can add, edit, duplicate, or delete custom exercises.

> [Screenshot 2: Library > Training > Exercises page showing the custom exercises list]

## Things to Note

- Built-in exercises are only browsable inside the Workout Builder left panel
- Custom exercises you create appear in both the Workout Builder and the Library > Training > Exercises page
- Built-in exercises are read-only. To modify one, duplicate it and edit the copy
- Video demonstrations are sourced from MuscleWiki for built-in exercises

## FAQs

### Where do I find the full exercise list?

Open any workout or programme in the Workout Builder. The left panel shows the complete exercise library with search and filter options.

### Do custom exercises appear in the Workout Builder?

Yes. Custom exercises appear alongside built-in exercises in the Workout Builder left panel.
