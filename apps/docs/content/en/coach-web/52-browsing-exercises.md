# Browsing Exercises

## What is this?

The exercise library in Athli contains a full searchable database of exercises. It includes thousands of built-in exercises with professional video demonstrations, as well as any custom exercises you have created. You can browse, search, and filter exercises to find exactly what you need when building workouts or exploring movement options for your clients.

## Why is it useful?

- Quickly find exercises by name without scrolling through the entire list
- Discover new exercises by browsing categories and muscle groups
- View video demonstrations and exercise details before adding them to a workout
- Exercise cards give you a quick overview of each movement at a glance
- The same browsing experience is available both in the library and in the exercise picker when building workouts

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Accessing the Exercise Library

1. Go to **Library** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Training** tab.
3. Click **Exercises**.

You will see the full list of exercises, including both built-in and custom exercises.

> [Screenshot: exercise library page showing the list of exercises with search bar at the top]

### Searching by Name

1. Click the **search bar** at the top of the exercise list.
2. Type the exercise name or a keyword (e.g., "squat", "bench", "curl").
3. Results update in real time as you type.
4. Click any exercise to view its details.

> [Screenshot: search bar with "deadlift" typed and filtered results showing below]

### Browsing Categories

Exercises are organized into categories such as strength, cardio, flexibility, and more. To browse by category:

1. Use the **category filter** in the filter panel.
2. Select one or more categories to narrow the list.
3. Browse the filtered results.

### Viewing Exercise Details

Click on any exercise to open its detail view. The detail view includes:

- **Exercise name**
- **Target muscles** (primary and secondary)
- **Equipment required** (barbell, dumbbell, machine, bodyweight, etc.)
- **Category** (strength, cardio, flexibility, etc.)
- **Video demonstration** showing proper form and technique

> [Screenshot: exercise detail view showing name, muscles, equipment, and video player]

### Understanding Exercise Cards

In the list view, each exercise card shows key information at a glance:

- **Name** of the exercise
- **Target muscles** displayed as tags
- **Equipment** needed for the movement

This lets you quickly scan and compare exercises without opening each one individually.

## Things to Note

- The exercise library is the same whether you access it from **Library > Training > Exercises** or from the exercise picker inside the workout builder.
- Built-in exercises are read-only. You cannot edit their name, muscles, or video. To modify a built-in exercise, duplicate it and edit the copy.
- Custom exercises you create appear alongside built-in exercises in the library.
- The search works on exercise names. For filtering by muscle group or equipment, use the filter panel.
- Video demonstrations are sourced from MuscleWiki for built-in exercises and play directly in the app.

## FAQs

### Can I search for exercises by muscle group?

The search bar works on exercise names. To find exercises by muscle group, use the filter panel to select the target muscle (e.g., chest, back, legs). See the article on filtering exercises for details.

### Do custom exercises appear in the same list?

Yes. Custom exercises are mixed in with built-in exercises and can be found using the same search and filter tools.

### Can I browse exercises while building a workout?

Yes. The exercise picker in the workout builder provides the same search, browse, and filter experience as the standalone exercise library.

### How many built-in exercises are there?

The library includes thousands of built-in exercises covering all major muscle groups, equipment types, and training categories.
