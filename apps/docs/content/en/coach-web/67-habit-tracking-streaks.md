# Tracking Habits and Streaks

## What is this?

Once habits are assigned, you can view each client's streaks, completion history, and adherence from a central location on their profile.

## Why is it useful?

Seeing habit progress at a glance helps you understand how consistent a client is outside of training. Streaks and adherence rates give you quick insight into who is staying on track and who might need extra support.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing Streaks and Progress

1. Go to the client's profile
2. Click the **Habits** tab
3. You see all assigned habits with their current streaks, adherence rates, and completion status in one place
4. Click on any habit to view its detailed completion calendar

> [Screenshot 1: Client Habits tab showing all assigned habits with streaks and adherence rates]

## Things to Note

- Each habit shows a streak count (consecutive days or weeks logged) and an adherence percentage
- The completion calendar highlights logged and missed days so you can spot patterns
- All habit logs are stored and remain accessible even after the habit duration ends

## FAQs

### Can clients see their own streaks?

Yes. Clients see their habit completion history and streaks in the mobile app.

### What counts as a streak?

A streak is the number of consecutive tracking periods (days or weeks) where the habit was logged without a gap.
