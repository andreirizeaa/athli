# Client App: Profile and Settings

## What is this?

The Profile tab lets you manage your personal details, view your coaching information, adjust app settings, and control notifications.

## Why it is useful

Keeping your profile up to date helps your coach personalize your training. Settings let you control how the app works for you, from notifications to security preferences.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Tap the **Profile** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. You see your name, profile picture, and coaching information
3. Below that are links to your goals, injuries, and personal details

> [Screenshot: Client profile screen showing name, photo, and info sections]

### Editing Your Profile

1. On the Profile tab, tap **Edit Profile**
2. Update your name or profile picture
3. Tap **Save**

> [Screenshot: Edit profile screen with name and photo fields]

### Viewing Your Goals and Injuries

1. On the Profile tab, tap **Goals** or **Injuries**
2. See the goals your coach has set or that you have discussed together
3. Injuries are tracked so your coach can program around them

> [Screenshot: Goals section showing active fitness goals]

### Managing Notifications

1. On the Profile tab, tap **Settings** or **Notifications**
2. Toggle push notifications on or off for different types:
   - New messages from your coach
   - Workout reminders
   - Check-in reminders
   - Habit reminders
3. Make sure notifications are also enabled in your device system settings

> [Screenshot: Notification settings with toggle switches for each type]

### Enabling Biometric Lock

1. Go to **Settings** from the Profile tab
2. Find the **Security** or **Biometric Lock** option
3. Enable Face ID or fingerprint lock
4. The app will require biometric verification when you open it

> [Screenshot: Security settings with biometric lock toggle]

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to **Settings > Security**
2. Tap **Change Password**
3. Enter your current password and your new password
4. Tap **Save**

> [Screenshot: Change password screen]

### Logging Out

1. Go to **Settings** from the Profile tab
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Tap **Log Out**
4. Confirm the action

> [Screenshot: Settings screen with log out button at bottom]

## Things to Note

- Your coach can also update some of your profile details from their side
- Keeping your timezone accurate helps with workout scheduling and check-in reminders
- Biometric lock adds an extra layer of privacy for your coaching data
- Logging out does not delete your data. You can log back in at any time

## Common Problems

**I cannot change my email address**
Email changes need to be verified. If you are having issues, contact your coach or support.

**Push notifications are not working**
Check both the in-app settings and your device system settings. Make sure Athli is allowed to send notifications. On Android, also check battery optimization settings as they can block notifications.

**I forgot my password**
On the login screen, tap **Forgot Password** and follow the reset steps.

## FAQs

### Can I delete my account?

Contact your coach or support to request account deletion.

### Can I change my coach?

Your account is linked to one coach. To switch coaches, your new coach would need to invite you to their account.

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is only visible to you and your coach. It is not shared with other clients or third parties.

### Can I use the app on multiple devices?

Yes. Log in with your credentials on any device and your data syncs automatically.

### What happens if my coach deactivates my account?

You will lose access to the app. Your data is preserved and can be restored if the coach reactivates your account.
