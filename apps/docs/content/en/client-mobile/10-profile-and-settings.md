# Client App: Profile and Settings

## What is this?

The Profile tab lets you manage your personal details, view your coach's profile, and adjust account security settings.

## Why it is useful

Keeping your profile up to date helps your coach personalize your training. The settings page also gives you access to security options and your coach's profile.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Tap the **Profile** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. You see your name, profile picture, and coaching information

> [Screenshot 1: Client profile screen showing name, photo, and info sections]

### Editing Your Details

1. On the Profile tab, tap **Edit Profile**
2. Update your name, profile picture, or other personal details
3. Tap **Save**

> [Screenshot 2: Edit profile screen with name and photo fields]

### Viewing Your Coach's Profile

1. On the Profile tab, tap your **coach's name** or profile card
2. View your coach's details and information

> [Screenshot 3: Coach profile view from the client app]

## Things to Note

- Your coach can also update some of your profile details from their side
- Keeping your timezone accurate helps with workout scheduling and check-in reminders

## FAQs

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is only visible to you and your coach. It is not shared with other clients or third parties.

### Can I use the app on multiple devices?

Yes. Log in with your credentials on any device and your data syncs automatically.
