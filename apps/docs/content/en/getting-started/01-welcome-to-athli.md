# Welcome to Athli

## What is Athli?

Athli is an all-in-one coaching platform that helps fitness coaches manage their clients, build training programs, track progress, and grow their business. Whether you coach in person, online, or a mix of both, Athli gives you everything you need in one place.

## Why use Athli?

Running a coaching business means juggling a lot of tools. Spreadsheets for programming, messaging apps for communication, payment platforms for billing, and random apps for tracking. Athli brings all of that together so you can focus on what matters: coaching.

## Who is Athli for?

- **Personal trainers** managing multiple clients
- **Online coaches** who need a professional client experience
- **Gym owners** who want to offer digital coaching alongside in-person sessions
- **Strength and conditioning coaches** who need detailed programming tools
- **Nutrition coaches** who want to combine training and nutrition in one platform

## Key Features

### For Coaches (Web and Mobile)
- **Client management** with profiles, goals, and injury tracking
- **Workout builder** with drag-and-drop exercises and AI generation
- **Training programs** with multi-week scheduling
- **Check-ins and questionnaires** to collect regular client feedback
- **Habit and metric tracking** for accountability
- **Progress photos** with side-by-side comparison
- **In-app messaging** with file sharing and reactions
- **AI assistant** that can create workouts, search clients, and analyze data
- **Automation flows** to handle missed check-ins and workout reminders
- **Business tools** with packages, coupons, and Stripe payments
- **Client onboarding flows** for a professional first impression

### For Clients (Mobile App)
- **Daily training view** with guided workout sessions
- **Progress tracking** with charts and history
- **Check-in submissions** with forms and ratings
- **Habit logging** for daily accountability
- **Direct messaging** with your coach
- **Progress photos** with comparison tools

## FAQs

### Is Athli available on desktop and mobile?

Yes. Coaches can use the web app on any browser and also have a mobile app. Clients use a dedicated mobile app available on iOS and Android.

### Can I try Athli for free?

Yes. Athli offers a free trial so you can explore all the features before committing to a plan.

### Is my data secure?

Yes. Athli uses industry-standard encryption, row-level security policies, and secure authentication. Your data and your clients' data are protected.

### Can I import my existing clients?

Yes. You can add clients one by one or upload a CSV file with multiple clients at once.
