# Understanding Athli Plans

## What is this?

Athli offers three subscription tiers designed for coaches at different stages of their business. Each plan includes a base number of clients, and you can add more clients or features as you grow.

## Plans Overview

### Starter (Free)

The Starter plan is completely free and perfect for coaches just getting started.

**Includes:**
- Coach Mobile App (iOS and Android)
- Client Mobile App (iOS and Android)
- Workouts and Programs
- Training Calendar
- Exercise Library (1,700+ exercises)
- Client Messaging
- Up to 5 clients

**Best for:** New coaches testing the platform or those with a small client base.

### Pro (From $20/month)

The Pro plan unlocks the tools most coaches need to run their business effectively.

**Everything in Starter, plus:**
- AI Workout Builder
- Custom Exercises
- Custom Workout Sections
- Questionnaires
- Check-ins
- Habits and Metrics Tracking
- 5GB On Demand Files

**Best for:** Growing coaches who want advanced training tools and client tracking.

### Max (From $99/month)

The Max plan is built for established coaches managing larger client bases.

**Everything in Pro, plus:**
- Unlimited On Demand Files
- Broadcast Messaging
- AI Todo List
- Priority Support
- Lower extra client pricing

**Best for:** Full-time coaches or coaching businesses with many active clients.

## Client Slots

Each plan comes with a base number of clients included. If you need more, you can add extra client slots:

- Select the number of clients you want on the pricing page using the client slider
- Extra clients are billed per client, per month
- Max plan offers lower per-client pricing than Pro

## Monthly vs Annual Billing

You can choose to pay monthly or annually:

- **Monthly:** Pay each month, cancel anytime
- **Annual:** Save 17% compared to monthly billing

Toggle between Monthly and Annual at the top of the pricing page to compare costs.

> [Screenshot 2: pricing page showing monthly/annual toggle]

## Add-ons

Add-ons let you unlock specific premium features on top of any paid plan:

### Automations
Automated onboarding sequences, custom trigger-based flows, scheduled actions and reminders, and multi-step automation chains.

### AI Assistant (Lyra)
Your all-round coaching assistant. Generate workouts and programs, track client progress, ask questions about your clients, and find clients who need attention.

### Payments
Collect payments from clients via Stripe. Includes packages and subscription management, automated payment collection, revenue analytics, and coupons.

Each add-on is billed separately and can be added or removed at any time from the billing page.

> [Screenshot 3: add-ons section on pricing page showing Automations, AI Assistant, and Payments]

## Free Trial

When you first sign up, you get a free trial that includes all features across all plans (including add-ons). This lets you explore everything before committing to a plan.

- Your trial countdown is shown on the billing page
- When the trial ends, you will need to select a plan to continue using premium features
- Starter plan features remain free forever

## Comparing Plans

To see a detailed feature-by-feature comparison:

1. Go to the pricing page (either from the marketing site or **Settings > Billing > Change Plan**)
2. Scroll down to **Compare Plans in Detail**
3. Review every feature and which plans include it

## FAQs

### Can I start on Starter and upgrade later?

Yes. You can upgrade to Pro or Max at any time. Your data and clients carry over.

### Do my clients pay anything?

No. The client mobile app is completely free. Only coaches pay for the platform.

### What counts as an active client?

Any client that is not archived counts toward your client limit. Archive inactive clients to free up slots.

### Can I try features before paying?

Yes. The free trial gives you access to all features so you can evaluate before choosing a plan.

### Are prices in USD?

Yes. All Athli pricing is in USD.
