# Assigning Forms to Clients on Mobile

## What is this?

Once you have built a check-in or questionnaire, you need to assign it to a client. The mobile app lets you assign forms from the client's profile, either by selecting existing templates from your library or by creating new ones on the spot.

## Why it is useful

You can assign forms to clients directly from your phone. Whether you are reviewing a client's profile and realize they need a new check-in, or you want to send a quick questionnaire, the assignment flow is fast and supports selecting multiple forms at once.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Check-ins

1. Go to a client's profile
2. Tap **Check-ins**
3. Tap the menu icon (top-right)
4. Select **Assign Check-in** to assign an existing template, or **Add Check-in** to create a new one directly for the client

**Assigning:** Browse or search your library, select one or more check-ins using the checkboxes, and tap the checkmark. The schedule (daily, weekly, etc.) is applied automatically from the template.

**Adding new:** Choose **New** or start from a **Template**, fill in the details (name, description, schedule), save to open the builder, add your questions, and save. This creates a form assigned directly to that client.

### Questionnaires

1. Go to a client's profile
2. Tap **Questionnaires**
3. Tap the menu icon (top-right)
4. Select **Assign Questionnaire** to assign an existing template, or **Add Questionnaire** to create a new one directly for the client

**Assigning:** Browse or search your library, select one or more questionnaires using the checkboxes, and tap the checkmark. The questionnaire starts in Draft status. You can send it to the client when ready.

**Adding new:** Choose **New** or start from a **Template**, fill in the details (name, description), save to open the builder, add your questions, and save. This creates a form assigned directly to that client.

> [Screenshot 2: Menu dropdown showing Assign Questionnaire and Add Questionnaire options]

## Things to Note

- You can assign multiple forms at once by selecting several checkboxes
- Check-in schedules are set on the template. The assignment applies the template's schedule to the client
- Questionnaires start in Draft status after assignment. Send them when you are ready
- The search bar in the assignment modal filters templates by name
- Assigned forms appear immediately in the client's profile

## FAQs

### Can I assign the same form to multiple clients?

Yes. Templates are reusable. Assign the same check-in or questionnaire to as many clients as you need.

### Can I change the schedule after assigning a check-in?

Yes. Open the check-in from the client's profile and edit the schedule in the settings.

### What happens when I assign a check-in?

The client starts receiving the check-in based on its schedule. They see it as a task on their home screen and receive push notifications when it is due.

### What happens when I assign a questionnaire?

It is created in Draft status. Once you send it, the client receives a notification and can fill it out.
