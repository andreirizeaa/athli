# Coach Mobile: Library

## What is this?

The Library tab on the coach mobile app gives you access to your workout and exercise library. Browse exercises, view exercise videos, and access your saved workouts.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Browsing Exercises

1. Tap the **Library** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. Browse or search for exercises by name
3. Filter by muscle group or equipment
4. Tap an exercise to see details and a video demonstration

> [Screenshot 1: Mobile exercise library with search and filters]

### Viewing Exercise Details

1. Tap any exercise from the library
2. See the target muscles, equipment needed, and category
3. Watch the demonstration video
4. Use this information when planning workouts for clients

> [Screenshot 2: Mobile exercise detail with video player]

### Accessing Workouts

1. In the Library, navigate to your saved workouts
2. Browse or search by name
3. Tap a workout to preview its exercises and structure

> [Screenshot 3: Mobile workout library list]

## Things to Note

- The exercise library contains thousands of exercises with videos
- Custom exercises you created on the web are also available here
- For creating new workouts or exercises, the web app provides a better experience

---

## FAQs

### Can I create exercises from the mobile app?

Exercise creation is best done on the web app where you have more editing tools.

### Are exercise videos available offline?

No. Videos require an internet connection to stream.
