# Coach Mobile: AI Assistant

## What is this?

The AI Assistant is available on the coach mobile app so you can get quick answers, create workouts, and search your client data from anywhere.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Opening the AI Assistant

1. Tap the **Assistant** option from the menu or home screen
2. You land on a chat interface
3. Type your question or request
4. The AI responds with context-aware answers

> [Screenshot 1: Mobile AI assistant chat screen]

### Example Requests

- "Create an upper body workout with dumbbells"
- "Show me clients who missed workouts this week"
- "What exercises target the glutes?"
- "How is Jake's squat progressing?"

### Working with AI Responses

1. Read the AI's response
2. If it suggests an action (like creating a workout), review the details
3. Confirm to execute the action or ask for modifications

> [Screenshot 2: Mobile AI response with action card]

## Things to Note

- The mobile AI assistant has the same capabilities as the web version
- Chat history syncs between web and mobile
- AI usage limits are shared across both platforms

---

## FAQs

### Does the AI have access to my client data on mobile?

Yes. The AI assistant works the same on mobile and web with access to your coaching data.

### Can I use voice input?

Voice input depends on your device's keyboard. The AI processes text input.
