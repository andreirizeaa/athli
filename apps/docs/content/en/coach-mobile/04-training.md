# Coach Mobile: Training

## What is this?

The Training tab on the coach mobile app shows a calendar view of all your clients' workouts for each day. You can see who has workouts scheduled, what they are doing, and whether they have completed them.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing the Training Calendar

1. Tap the **Training** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. You see a daily view of all client workouts
3. Swipe left/right to navigate between days
4. Each client's workout is shown as a card with status indicators

> [Screenshot: Mobile training calendar showing all clients for a day]

### Previewing a Workout

1. Tap any workout card
2. A preview shows all exercises, sets, reps, and notes
3. You can see if the client has completed the workout and their logged results

> [Screenshot: Mobile workout preview with exercise details]

### Reviewing Completed Workouts

1. Completed workouts show a checkmark indicator
2. Tap to see the client's logged sets, reps, and weights
3. View post-workout metrics and notes the client added

> [Screenshot: Completed workout review with client-logged data]

## Things to Note

- The training tab shows workouts across all clients for the selected day
- For a single client's training calendar, go to their profile and tap Training
- Use the web app for building complex programs and workouts

---

## FAQs

### Can I reassign workouts from mobile?

You can add workouts to a client's day. For moving workouts between days, the web app is easier.

### Can I see workout completion in real time?

Yes. When a client completes a workout, their card updates immediately.
