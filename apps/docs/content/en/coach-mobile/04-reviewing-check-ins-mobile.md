# Reviewing Check-ins on Mobile

## What is this?

When a client submits a check-in that requires review, the coach can review it directly from the mobile app. The review flow lets you read through the client's answers, leave a coach comment, and mark the check-in as reviewed — all from your phone.

## Why it is useful

You can stay on top of client check-ins without needing to open the web app. When a check-in is submitted, you can review it immediately from the home page shortcut or from the client's profile. Once you mark it as reviewed, the client receives a push notification with your comment.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Find Check-ins to Review (Home Page)

From the **Home** page, tap **Today's Forms** to see check-ins that need your attention. Items marked with a "Needs Review" pill are waiting for your review.

> [Screenshot 1: Home page showing Today's Forms shortcut with check-ins that need review]

### Step 2: Find Check-ins to Review (Client Profile)

Alternatively, go to a specific client's profile and tap into their **Check-ins** section. The submissions list shows each submission with a status pill — look for "Needs Review".

> [Screenshot 2: Client check-in submissions list showing status pills including "Needs Review"]

### Step 3: Review and Comment

1. Tap a submission to open the form review screen
2. Read through the client's answers
3. At the bottom of the date card, tap **Mark as Reviewed**
4. A modal opens with a text area for your coach comment
5. Write your feedback and tap the checkmark to submit

Once submitted, the check-in status changes to "Reviewed" and the client receives a push notification with the check-in name and your comment.

> [Screenshot 3: Form review screen showing the date card with "Mark as Reviewed" button and the review modal]

## Things to Note

- Only check-ins with a "Needs Review" status show the review button
- The review button appears inside the date card at the top of the form review screen
- After submitting, both the review modal and the form review screen close automatically
- The client receives a push notification titled "[Check-in name] was reviewed" with your comment as the body
- The submissions list updates to show the new "Reviewed" status

---

## FAQs

### Can I edit a review after submitting?

No. Once a review is submitted, the comment is saved and cannot be modified from the mobile app.

### Does the client see my comment?

Yes. The coach comment is visible to the client on their form review screen, and the comment is also sent as a push notification.

### What happens if a check-in does not need review?

Check-ins without the review requirement show as "Completed" and do not display the review button.
