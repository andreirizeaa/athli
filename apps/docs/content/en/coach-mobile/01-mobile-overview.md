# Coach Mobile App Overview

## What is this?

The Athli Coach mobile app gives you access to your coaching tools on the go. You can manage clients, review workouts, message athletes, and check your schedule from your phone.

## Why it is useful

Coaching does not stop when you leave your desk. The mobile app lets you stay connected with clients, review their workouts between sessions, and handle quick tasks without needing a computer.

## Use Cases

- Checking a client's workout completion between gym sessions
- Responding to client messages on the go
- Reviewing check-in submissions during your commute
- Quickly looking up a client's profile before an in-person session

## Things to Note

- Some advanced features like the program builder and business packages are only available on the web
- The mobile app syncs in real time with the web app
- Push notifications are managed through the mobile app's settings
- Download the app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android)

## FAQs

### Do I need both the web and mobile app?

No. You can use either one. However, the web app has more advanced features. The mobile app is best for quick tasks and staying connected on the go.

### Is my data the same on both?

Yes. Everything syncs in real time between web and mobile.

### Can I build workouts on mobile?

You can view and make basic edits to workouts on mobile. For building complex workouts with sections and drag-and-drop, the web app is recommended.
