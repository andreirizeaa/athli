# Coach Mobile: Client Management

## What is this?

The Clients tab on the coach mobile app lets you view your roster, access client profiles, and manage their training, check-ins, habits, metrics, and more.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing Your Client List

1. Tap the **Clients** tab at the bottom of the screen
2. You see all your active clients
3. Use the search bar to find a specific client
4. Tap a client to open their profile

> [Screenshot: Mobile clients tab with client list and search]

### Navigating a Client Profile

Once you tap a client, you can access:

- **Overview** with bio, goals, and injuries
- **Training** calendar with assigned workouts
- **Check-ins** and submissions
- **Habits** and completion logs
- **Metrics** with charts
- **Photos** for progress tracking
- **Exercise History** with performance data
- **Files** shared with the client
- **Notes** for private coaching notes

> [Screenshot: Mobile client profile with navigation options]

### Editing Client Details

1. Open a client's profile
2. Tap **Edit Details**
3. Update their name, category, goals, or other information
4. Tap **Save**

> [Screenshot: Mobile edit client details screen]

### Viewing a Client's Training Calendar

1. Open a client's profile
2. Tap **Training**
3. Navigate between weeks using the arrows
4. Tap any day to see the assigned workout
5. Tap a workout to preview it or add a new one

> [Screenshot: Mobile training calendar with weekly view]

### Assigning a Workout to a Day

1. On the training calendar, tap an empty day
2. Choose to assign a workout from your library or create one
3. Select the workout and confirm

> [Screenshot: Mobile add workout to day dialog]

## Things to Note

- All changes sync instantly with the web app
- Complex workout building (sections, drag-and-drop) is better done on web
- You can view exercise history charts for each client to track progress

---

## FAQs

### Can I add new clients from the mobile app?

This may be available through the add button. For bulk uploads and invite links, use the web app.

### Can I archive clients from mobile?

Client archiving is available through the client's settings on their profile.
