# Archived Chats

The archived chats feature lets you clean up your chat list by archiving conversations you do not need to see regularly, without deleting them.

## What It Is

A way to move chats out of your main chat list into a separate archived section. Archived chats are still accessible whenever you need them, but they will not clutter your active conversations.

## Why It Is Useful

- Keep your chat list focused on active conversations
- Reduce visual clutter without losing any messages
- Quickly find important chats without scrolling past inactive ones

## How to Archive a Chat

1. Open the **Chats** tab.
2. Swipe left on a conversation (or use the menu).
3. Tap **Archive**.

The chat will move to the archived section.

## Viewing Archived Chats

1. Open the **Chats** tab.
2. Tap **Archived** at the top of the chat list.
3. Browse your archived conversations.

> [Screenshot: archived chats list]

## Unarchiving a Chat

1. Go to the **Archived** section.
2. Open the archived chat.
3. Send a message, or use the menu to unarchive.

The chat will return to your main chat list.

## FAQs

**Do I still receive messages from archived chats?**
Yes. If someone sends a message to an archived chat, you will still receive notifications. The chat may automatically move back to your main list.

**Can coaches see that I archived our chat?**
No. Archiving is local to your device. Your coach has no way to know if you archived the conversation.

**Is there a limit to how many chats I can archive?**
No. You can archive as many chats as you want.

**Does archiving delete any messages?**
No. All messages are preserved. Archiving only hides the chat from your main list.
