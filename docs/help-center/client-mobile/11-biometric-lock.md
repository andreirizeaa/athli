# Biometric Lock

The biometric lock feature adds an extra layer of security to the Athli mobile app. When enabled, the app requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode to open.

## What It Is

A security feature that locks the Athli app behind your device's biometric authentication (fingerprint or face recognition) or passcode. Every time you open the app, you will need to authenticate before accessing your data.

## Why It Is Useful

- Protect sensitive client data if someone picks up your phone
- Keep coaching conversations and client information private
- Peace of mind when your phone is shared or left unattended

## How to Enable It

1. Open the Athli mobile app.
2. Go to **Settings > Preferences**.
3. Toggle on **Biometric Lock**.
4. Confirm with your fingerprint or face scan.

> [Screenshot: settings preferences screen with biometric lock toggle]

## How It Works

Once enabled:
- The app will show a lock screen every time you open it or switch back to it.
- Authenticate using Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.
- If authentication fails, the app remains locked.

> [Screenshot: biometric lock screen prompting for Face ID]

## Disabling Biometric Lock

1. Go to **Settings > Preferences**.
2. Toggle off **Biometric Lock**.
3. Confirm with your biometric or passcode.

## FAQs

**What if my device does not support biometrics?**
If your device does not have Face ID or Touch ID, the app will fall back to your device passcode.

**Does this protect individual chats or just the whole app?**
Biometric lock protects the entire app. All screens are locked until you authenticate.

**Will I need to authenticate every time I switch apps?**
Yes. When you leave the app and come back, you will be prompted to authenticate again.

**What if biometric authentication stops working?**
You can always use your device passcode as a fallback. If issues persist, try toggling the setting off and on again, or check your device's biometric settings.
