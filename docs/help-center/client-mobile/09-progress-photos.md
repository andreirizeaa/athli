# Client App: Progress Photos

## What is this?

Progress photos let you take and upload photos on a regular basis to visually document your transformation. Your coach can view your photos and compare them across different dates.

## Why it is useful

The scale does not always reflect reality. You might be building muscle while losing fat, which means the number stays the same but your body looks completely different. Progress photos capture changes that numbers miss and are one of the most motivating tools in your fitness journey.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Taking a Progress Photo

1. Navigate to the **Progress Photos** section
2. Tap **Take Photo** or **Add Photo**
3. Use your camera to take a new photo, or select one from your photo library
4. The photo is saved and tagged with today's date
5. Tap **Save**

> [Screenshot: Progress photo capture screen with camera and library options]

### Viewing Your Photos

1. Go to the Progress Photos section
2. You see your photos organized by date
3. Tap any date to view the photos from that day
4. Swipe through multiple photos if you took more than one

> [Screenshot: Photo timeline showing photos organized by date]

### Comparing Photos

1. In the Progress Photos section, tap **Compare**
2. Select two dates to compare side by side
3. Your photos from each date appear next to each other
4. This makes it easy to see visual changes over time

> [Screenshot: Side-by-side photo comparison with two different dates]

### Tips for Better Progress Photos

- Take photos at the same time of day (morning is best)
- Use the same lighting and background each time
- Wear the same or similar clothing
- Take front, side, and back shots for a full picture
- Stay consistent with your photo schedule (weekly or biweekly works well)

## Things to Note

- Your photos are private and only visible to you and your coach
- Photos are stored securely in the cloud
- Try to keep a consistent schedule for the most useful comparisons
- Image files should be under 5MB

## Common Problems

**My photo is not uploading**
Check your internet connection and make sure the file is under 5MB. Try taking a new photo with the in-app camera.

**I want to delete a photo**
Open the photo and look for the delete option. Be careful as this cannot be undone.

**The comparison does not look right**
Make sure you are comparing photos taken in similar conditions (lighting, pose, clothing).

## FAQs

**Can my coach see all my progress photos?**
Yes. Your coach has access to your photos for tracking purposes.

**How often should I take progress photos?**
Once a week or every two weeks is a good frequency. Monthly is fine for slower body composition changes.

**Can I add photos from my camera roll?**
Yes. You can select existing photos from your device when adding a progress photo.

**Are my photos backed up?**
Yes. Photos are stored in the cloud and accessible even if you switch devices.
