# Nutrition: Recipes, Ingredients, and Recipe Books

Athli includes a nutrition management system in your library where you can create and organize recipes, ingredients, and recipe books for your clients.

> **Note:** The nutrition feature is currently in development. This article will be updated as the feature becomes fully available.

## What It Is

The nutrition library lets you build a collection of recipes, manage individual ingredients with nutritional data, and organize everything into recipe books that you can share with clients.

## Where to Find It

1. Go to **Library** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Nutrition** tab.
3. You will see three sections: **Recipes**, **Ingredients**, and **Recipe Books**.

> [Screenshot: nutrition library overview showing the three tabs]

## Recipes

Recipes are individual meals or dishes that you create for your clients. Each recipe includes:

- A name and description
- A list of ingredients with quantities
- Preparation instructions
- Nutritional breakdown (calculated automatically from ingredients)

### Creating a Recipe

1. Go to **Library > Nutrition > Recipes**.
2. Click **Add Recipe**.
3. Fill in the recipe details: name, description, and instructions.
4. Add ingredients from your ingredient library and specify quantities.
5. Save the recipe.

> [Screenshot: recipe creation form with ingredients added]

## Ingredients

Ingredients are the building blocks of your recipes. Each ingredient stores nutritional information (calories, protein, carbs, fat) so that recipe nutrition is calculated automatically.

### Adding an Ingredient

1. Go to **Library > Nutrition > Ingredients**.
2. Click **Add Ingredient**.
3. Enter the ingredient name, serving size, and nutritional values.
4. Save.

> [Screenshot: ingredient form with nutritional fields]

## Recipe Books

Recipe books let you group related recipes into collections. For example, you might create a "High Protein Meals" book or a "Vegetarian Options" book.

### Creating a Recipe Book

1. Go to **Library > Nutrition > Recipe Books**.
2. Click **Add Recipe Book**.
3. Give it a name and description.
4. Add recipes from your library to the book.
5. Save.

> [Screenshot: recipe book with recipes listed inside]

## FAQs

**Can I assign recipes directly to clients?**
This functionality is coming soon. Currently you can build your library in preparation.

**Are nutritional values calculated automatically?**
Yes, once you add ingredients with nutritional data to a recipe, the totals are calculated automatically based on the quantities you specify.

**Can clients see my recipe library?**
Recipe sharing with clients will be available when the full nutrition feature launches.
