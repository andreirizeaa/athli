# Cancelling and Reactivating Your Subscription

## What is this?

This guide explains how to cancel your Athli subscription, what happens when you cancel, and how to reactivate if you change your mind.

## Cancelling Your Subscription

### How to Cancel

1. Go to **Settings > Account > Danger Zone** (or **Settings > Billing**).
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. A confirmation dialog will appear explaining what happens next.
4. Confirm the cancellation.

> [Screenshot: cancel subscription confirmation dialog]

### What Happens When You Cancel

- Your subscription is **not cancelled immediately**. It is scheduled for cancellation at the end of your current billing period.
- You keep full access to all your plan features until the cancellation date.
- The billing page will show the cancellation date so you know exactly when access changes.
- No further charges will be made after the current period.

### After Cancellation Takes Effect

- You drop down to the **Starter plan** (free).
- Starter features remain available: workouts, programs, exercise library, messaging, up to 5 clients.
- Premium features (check-ins, questionnaires, habits, metrics, AI tools, add-ons) become unavailable.
- **Your data is NOT deleted.** Everything is preserved. If you resubscribe, all your data will be there exactly as you left it.
- If you have more than 5 active clients, you will need to archive clients to get within the Starter limit.

## Reactivating Your Subscription

If you cancelled but your billing period has not ended yet, you can undo the cancellation:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. You will see a notice that your subscription is scheduled for cancellation.
3. Click **Reactivate**.
4. Your subscription continues as normal, and the cancellation is undone.
5. Billing resumes on the regular schedule.

> [Screenshot: billing page showing reactivate option for a subscription scheduled for cancellation]

### Resubscribing After Cancellation

If your subscription has already ended:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Click **Change Plan**.
3. Select your desired plan, client count, and add-ons.
4. Complete checkout through Stripe.
5. Your plan is activated immediately and all your previous data is still there.

## Cancelling Individual Add-ons

You can cancel add-ons without cancelling your entire subscription:

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Find the add-on you want to remove.
3. Click the cancel option next to it.
4. The add-on is scheduled for removal at the end of your billing period.
5. You keep access to the add-on until then.

To undo an add-on cancellation, click **Reactivate** next to the add-on before the billing period ends.

## Deleting Your Account

Account deletion is separate from subscription cancellation:

1. Go to **Settings > Account > Danger Zone**.
2. You must cancel your subscription first if you have an active paid plan.
3. Click **Delete Account**.
4. Type the confirmation text to proceed.
5. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

**Warning:** Account deletion is permanent. Make sure you have exported any data you need before proceeding.

## FAQs

**Will I be charged after cancelling?**
No. Once you cancel, no further charges are made. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

**Can I cancel during my free trial?**
If you are on a free trial, it simply expires at the end of the trial period. No payment is taken.

**What if I cancel by accident?**
Click **Reactivate** on the billing page before your billing period ends to undo the cancellation.

**How long is my data kept after cancelling?**
Your data is preserved indefinitely. You can resubscribe at any time and pick up where you left off.

**Can I cancel from the mobile app?**
Yes. Go to Settings > Billing in the mobile app, which opens the billing page where you can manage your subscription.

**Do my clients lose access when I cancel?**
Your clients keep the free Starter-level experience. Premium features you assigned (check-ins, habits, metrics) become unavailable to them as well.
