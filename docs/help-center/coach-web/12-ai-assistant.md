# AI Assistant: Complete Guide

## What is this?

The AI Assistant is an intelligent coaching helper built into Athli. It understands your client data, workout library, and coaching business. You can chat with it in natural language to create workouts, search clients, analyze progress, draft messages, and much more. Think of it as a senior coaching assistant that knows everything about your clients and can take action on your behalf.

## Why it is useful

Instead of clicking through multiple pages to build a workout, look up a client's metrics, or find who has been inactive, you can simply ask. The AI saves you time on repetitive tasks and gives you quick access to insights across your entire coaching business.

## Where to find it

- **Web app**: Click **Assistant** in the sidebar for the full chat page, or click the AI icon in the top navigation for a side panel
- **Mobile app**: Tap the **Assistant** option from the menu

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Starting a New Chat

1. Click **Assistant** in the sidebar (web) or tap **Assistant** (mobile)
2. You land on a new empty conversation
3. Type your question or request and press Enter
4. The AI processes your request and responds

> [Screenshot: Empty assistant page with capability overview cards]

### Understanding Action Cards

When the AI proposes an action (like creating a workout or assigning a metric), it shows an **action card**:

1. The card shows the full details of what will be created or changed
2. Review the content carefully
3. Click **Confirm** to execute the action
4. Click **Edit** to modify the details before confirming
5. Click **Dismiss** to cancel

Action cards appear for:
- Creating workouts and sections
- Assigning workouts to clients
- Drafting messages
- Creating check-in templates and metrics
- Adding goals and injuries
- Assigning metrics to clients

> [Screenshot: Action card for a generated workout showing exercises with confirm and edit buttons]

### Chat History

1. The sidebar shows all your past conversations
2. Each chat has an AI-generated title summarizing the topic
3. Click any previous chat to continue where you left off
4. Click **New** to start a fresh conversation
5. Use the search bar to find specific chats by title
6. Delete chats you no longer need with the trash icon

> [Screenshot: Chat sidebar showing conversation history with titles and dates]

### Using the Side Panel (Web Only)

1. Click the **AI icon** in the top navigation bar
2. A chat panel opens on the right side of the screen
3. The AI has context about the page you are currently viewing
4. Ask questions related to what is on screen
5. This is useful for quick questions without leaving your current page

> [Screenshot: Side panel assistant open while viewing a client's training page]

### Viewing Tool Activity

When the AI is working on your request, you can see what it is doing:

1. Tool indicators appear showing which data the AI is looking up
2. For example: "Searching clients...", "Fetching workout data...", "Analyzing metrics..."
3. This transparency helps you understand how the AI arrives at its answer

## Example Conversations

### Quick Workout Creation
**You**: "Create a full body workout for a beginner, 6 exercises, 3 sets each, bodyweight only"
**AI**: Creates a workout card with exercises like squats, push-ups, lunges, planks, rows, and glute bridges with appropriate rep ranges.

### Client Check-up
**You**: "How is Sarah doing? Anything I should worry about?"
**AI**: Pulls Sarah's recent workout completions, check-in responses, habit adherence, and metric trends. Highlights any concerns like declining completion rates or missed check-ins.

### End of Week Review
**You**: "Which clients missed workouts this week?"
**AI**: Lists all clients who have incomplete workouts with the number of missed sessions.

### Program Planning
**You**: "Search for exercises that target the posterior chain using a barbell"
**AI**: Returns a list of matching exercises from the catalog with descriptions and muscle groups.

## Things to Note

- The AI has read access to all your coaching data (clients, workouts, metrics, check-ins, habits, exercises)
- It can create and assign content through action cards that require your confirmation
- It does not have access to your messages, billing information, or private notes
- Chat history is saved and persists across sessions
- AI usage may be limited based on your plan
- The AI generates a short title for each conversation automatically

## Common Problems

**The AI does not understand my request**
Try being more specific. Include details like client names, exercise names, number of sets, or date ranges.

**The AI gave incorrect data about a client**
The AI uses the data available in your account. If something seems wrong, verify the data directly on the client's profile page.

**Action cards are not appearing**
Not all requests result in actions. The AI shows action cards only when it detects something actionable. Try phrasing your request as a command: "Create a workout..." rather than "Can you tell me about workouts?"

**The AI is slow to respond**
Complex requests that involve looking up data for multiple clients may take a few seconds. The tool activity indicators show what the AI is working on.

**My chat history disappeared**
Make sure you are logged into the correct account. Chat history is tied to your user account.

## FAQs

**Is the AI assistant available on mobile?**
Yes. The AI Assistant works on both web and the coach mobile app with the same capabilities.

**Does the AI learn from my coaching style?**
The AI uses your client data and coaching context for each conversation. It does not permanently "learn" from past interactions, but your chat history is available for reference.

**Can the AI make changes without my permission?**
No. Every action requires your explicit confirmation through an action card. The AI cannot modify data without you clicking Confirm.

**Does the AI have access to my billing information?**
No. The AI only accesses coaching-related data.

**Can I undo an action the AI performed?**
Once confirmed, you would need to manually reverse the change (delete the workout, remove the goal, etc.).

**Is there a message length limit?**
Yes. Messages are limited to 10,000 characters. This is more than enough for any coaching request.

**Can the AI create training programs?**
The AI can create individual workouts and sections. Full multi-week program creation is planned for the future.

**Can the AI send messages to my clients directly?**
The AI drafts messages for you to review. You confirm before anything is sent.

**Is my conversation data private?**
Yes. Your AI conversations are only accessible by you. They are not shared with other users or used to train AI models.

**What happens if I am offline?**
The AI requires an internet connection to process requests. You cannot use it offline.
