# Coach Mobile: Home and Dashboard

## What is this?

The Home screen on the coach mobile app shows you a summary of your day. See today's client workouts, review at-risk clients, and manage your to-do list.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Viewing Today's Workouts

1. Open the app and you land on the **Home** tab
2. Scroll through the workout cards to see each client's scheduled workout
3. Tap a card to view workout details, completion status, and client feedback

> [Screenshot: Mobile home screen showing workout cards for today]

### Checking At-Risk Clients

1. On the Home screen, look for the at-risk section
2. This highlights clients who have missed workouts or check-ins
3. Tap a client to go directly to their profile

> [Screenshot: At-risk clients section on mobile home]

### Managing Your To-Do List

1. Tap the **To-Do** option from the Home screen
2. View automated tasks and your personal task list
3. Tap to complete tasks or add new ones

> [Screenshot: Mobile to-do list with task items]

## FAQs

**Is the mobile dashboard the same as the web dashboard?**
It shows similar information in a mobile-friendly layout. Some details may be condensed for the smaller screen.

**Can I customize what appears on the home screen?**
The home screen layout is fixed to show the most relevant daily information.
